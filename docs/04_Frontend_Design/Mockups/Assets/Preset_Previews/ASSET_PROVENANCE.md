# VisualPrompt Studio — Preset Preview Asset Provenance

**Status:** Current disclosure for Stage 6 v1.4  
**Created:** 10 October 2026  
**Generation method:** built-in AI raster image generation  
**Format:** six standalone landscape PNG files with opaque backgrounds

## What these images are

The six current Preset preview images are AI-created creative-direction references used by Home, Built-in and Build. They were not found or downloaded online. They communicate composition, light, material and mood; they do not guarantee a particular model output.

## What these images are not

They were generated from direct art-direction prompts, not by running the complete DISC-01 Preset molds, field catalogue, compiler template or validation pipeline. They therefore validate the UI's image-led presentation, not the product's Prompt compilation fidelity.
